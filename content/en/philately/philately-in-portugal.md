Philately also grows through meetings, reading and exchanging information. This initial selection brings together websites for exploring Portuguese collecting, finding material and learning more. Entries are grouped by resource type, without a commercial ranking. Most linked pages are in Portuguese, although some offer English content.

## Organisations and postal issues

- [Federação Portuguesa de Filatelia — Portuguese Philatelic Federation](https://fpfilatelia.wordpress.com/). News, exhibitions and federation information. Its [club directory](https://fpfilatelia.wordpress.com/clubes/) helps you discover other associations and groups around the country; confirm contact details directly with each organisation.
- [CTT — Philately](https://www.ctt.pt/particulares/filatelia/). The postal operator's information on stamp issues and publications. See also the [monthly releases](https://www.ctt.pt/particulares/filatelia/lancamentos-do-mes), which link to information sheets providing context for issues.

## Associations and local groups

- [Clube Filatélico de Portugal](https://www.cfportugal.pt/) — Lisbon. Information about the club and its activities. This address currently redirects to its Squarespace website.
- [Secção Filatélica da Associação Académica de Coimbra](https://academica.pt/orgaos/seccoes-culturais/sfilatelica-aac-seccao-filatelica/) — Coimbra. An institutional introduction to a section devoted to philately, commemorative postmarks and other forms of collecting, with links to its channels.
- [Núcleo Filatélico do Ateneu Comercial do Porto](https://live.nfacp.pt/) — Porto. The group's portal, including access to its auction platform. Some functions depend on JavaScript or registration.

To explore other regions, use the Federation's directory above. Inclusion on this page does not imply an affiliation or partnership with the NFB.

## Dealers and collecting supplies

- [Filatelia Maçãs](https://filatelia-macas.com/quem-somos) — Lisbon. A philatelic dealer offering stamps from Portugal and former Portuguese colonies. Its website also lists CTT books and catalogues.
- [Stamps Portugal](https://www.stampsportugal.com/). An online stamp shop with Portuguese stamps and other philatelic categories. A starting point for exploring issues and comparing descriptions.

## Auctions and lot research

- [Frazão Auctions — Philately Portugal](https://www.frazaoauctions.com/). An auction house specialising in the philately of Portugal and former Portuguese colonies. Catalogues provide opportunities to examine items and their descriptions.
- [Portal do Colecionador](https://www.portaldocolecionador.com/). An auction platform offering philately and numismatics, with catalogues and past results.

Before bidding, read the full description, examine the images and check the sale conditions. The hammer price may exclude buyer's commission, taxes or delivery. These links are informational references, not seller assessments or purchasing recommendations.

## Reading and research

- [Filatelicamente — Clube Nacional de Filatelia, University of Porto archive](https://www.up.pt/arquivoweb/www.fep.up.pt/docentes/cpimenta/lazer/WebFilatelicamente/public_html/index.html). A record of the club's publishing activity, with references to journals, catalogues and books. This is a historical archive, not a current events guide; some older links may be unavailable.
- [Philatelic literature](/en/philately/philatelic-literature/). Our annotated selection of readings and tools for beginning your research.
