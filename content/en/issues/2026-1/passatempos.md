Choose an item from your collection and describe it without naming its subject. Use just three clues: a shape, a colour and a word.

Ask someone else to find the item among a small selection. Then swap roles. The aim is to discuss the chosen details, without competition or scoring.

**Variation:** draw just one detail and see whether it is enough to recognise the image.
