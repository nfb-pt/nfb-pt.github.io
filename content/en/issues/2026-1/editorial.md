A publication can bring together people who see the same objects in different ways. Between an image, a note and a conversation, there is room for a question we cannot yet answer.

This demonstration issue takes that as its starting point. The texts explore looking at a stamp, the relationship between a letter and its context, and how a collection can follow our interests.

It does not present a historical issue or replace members’ voices. These pages show a structure open to future contributions, with clear authorship and room for images, references and further reading.

May each new issue invite us to look again — and share what we discover.
