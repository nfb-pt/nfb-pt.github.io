Looking at a city with curiosity can begin with simple gestures: noticing a shape, drawing an outline or writing down a word encountered along the way.

A future page about Braga could connect such observations with philatelic material related to the city. Photographs, stamps and documents should include confirmed identification and credits.

In this issue, the illustration is an original composition of imaginary stamps. Its stylised arch suggests a graphic reading of the city; it does not document a postal issue or identify a particular monument.

The invitation remains: which detail of Braga would you like to see accompanied by a philatelic story?
