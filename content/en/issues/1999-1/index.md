The **March 1999** issue, **volume 4, number 1**, is available as a scan of its eight original pages. The editorial introduces a new bulletin format and proposes extending its coverage to other forms of collecting alongside philately.

[Download the complete issue — Portuguese PDF, 8 pages, 4.8 MB](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf). This page provides an English summary; the scanned publication is in Portuguese.

## Annotated contents

1. **[Page 1 — Editorial](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf#page=1).** The bulletin's aims for 1999 and an invitation for members to contribute. Also includes an announcement of the Primavera 99 exhibition, a note about advertising and the issue's contents.
2. **[Page 2 — Association activities](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf#page=2).** Announcement of Primavera 99 at Casa dos Crivos, results from LACOBRIPEX 98, a stamp collecting school led by João Machado and a mention of the monthly collectors' fair.
3. **[Page 3 — The first stamp issues of 1999](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf#page=3).** Articles on the “Profissões e Personagens do Século XIX” series, the “O Amolador” postage label and the introduction of dual escudo/euro denominations. Includes the announced stamp programme through the end of May.
4. **[Page 4 — Philatelic curiosities](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf#page=4).** A piece about the 20-escudo stamp from the 1953 Portuguese postage stamp centenary issue, depicting Queen Maria II, and further philatelic anecdotes. The page credits _100 Selos Famosos_, by Eurico Carlos E. Lage Cardoso, as its source.
5. **[Page 5 — Other forms of collecting](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf#page=5).** Fernando Ferreira's collection of advertising glasses and Carlos Alberto Martins Portas's miniature agricultural machinery and implements. The texts are credited to the CTT magazine _Clube do Coleccionador_.
6. **[Page 6 — Our members](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf#page=6).** A profile of João Machado on his 81st birthday, recalling his collecting, teaching and involvement in the association's beginnings and activities.
7. **[Page 7 — Puzzles](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf#page=7).** A word puzzle built around “FILATELIA” and a crossword with across and down clues.
8. **[Page 8 — Closing page](/documents/a-pagina/1999/1/APagina-Ano-4-Num-1.pdf#page=8).** The poem “Lágrimas por chorar”, signed Cruz Lopes, followed by notices about the premises, access to philatelic material and the fundraising initiative labelled “TOTOSELO” in the original.
