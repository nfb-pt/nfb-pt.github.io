**A Página do NFB** brings together philately, collecting, association life and the city of Braga.

This archive will bring together digitised paper issues and new digital editions. Each issue may include an editorial, activities, articles, information for members, curiosities, puzzles, a view of Braga and poetry.

Demonstration issues are labelled **DEMO**. They do not represent issues published by the association.
